About the day

A full day of building, not just listening.

Hacktoberfest 2026 is all about building with open-weight models and open-source AI. This Hack Day is our local Fest: starter templates, mentors on the floor all day, and a curated set of problem statements. Bring your own idea if you'd rather.

You'll form a team, build something, and show it to the room before the day ends. Every tool and model we use is free, and the coding projects run in Google Colab, so there's nothing to install.

What to bring

  • Your laptop (with a web browser)
  • Your charger
  • Curiosity. Everything else is covered.
  • A Google account (for Colab and Sheets)

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Problems 1 and 2 need no coding at all, and Problem 3 only asks you to edit text and settings in a chatbot that already works. Mentors are on the floor all day.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop with a web browser, a charger and curiosity. You'll also need a Google account. Problems 2, 3 and 5 use a free Groq account or API key.

Can I come alone or bring my own idea?

Yes to both. Teams (2–4 people) are formed during the 11:30 AM Team Building slot, and you can propose your own open-source-AI idea instead of a listed problem.

Is it free?

Hacktoberfest is free for everyone, and all models and tools used on the day are free.

Do pull requests count towards swag?

Not this year. Hacktoberfest 2026 moved to learning and building together, so rewards come from attending Fests and online activities rather than PRs. You're still very welcome to contribute to open source and share your work.

What happens at the showcase?

Every team demos a working project to the room (5:30 PM). Each problem statement lists exactly what to present, for example a live demo plus before-and-after results.
